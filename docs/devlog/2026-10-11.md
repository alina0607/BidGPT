## 2. Cat-painting transformer: the trial

### Code

`sketch/brushgpt.py`: data (`data`), training (`train`) and evaluation (`eval`); `data/kaggle/brushgpt/` holds the
Kaggle dataset `alinachuang/cat-brush-gpt` and kernel `cat-brush-gpt-trial`.

- A painting is `brushtokens.encode` of the four face stages: 3,046 tokens (BOS, 4 stage markers, 608 strokes × 5,
  EOS); vocabulary 7,499 (HANDOFF said 7,531; 7,499 is what the code gives). decode → encode returns the same tokens.
- Mirror images in token space: x bin i → 255 − i, angle bin j → 31 − j; mirroring twice gives the painting back.
  The bristle streaks depend on x, so a mirrored painting renders 0.005 (mean absolute, 0–1) away from the mirrored
  render.
- Splits: AFHQ's test folder is the test set (none painted yet); of AFHQ train, a stable 8% by name hash is
  validation. 891 paintings: 809 train, 82 val.
- `llm/model.py`: `generate(preallocate=True)` writes the KV cache into buffers of the final length instead of
  concatenating every step (which copied the whole cache per token; fine at 257 tokens, not at 3,046). Same tokens
  as before (new test, greedy and sampled). Default path unchanged.
- The copy check compares each generated painting with every training painting **and its mirror image**: without
  the mirror, a copied mirrored training cat measured 0.11 away, as far as an unseen cat, and was missed. With it:
  mirrored copy 0.003, exact copy 0.0, unseen cats 0.11–0.17.
- Watchdogs (as in `paint_afhq.py`) on training intervals, sampling batches and rendering chunks: stop if the recent
  median is over 2.5× the first ones; GPU memory logged each time.
- Tests: `tests/test_brushgpt.py` (layout, mirror, split, watchdog). CUPID on brushgpt.py + model.py: 22 → 12
  findings (left: model.py's existing annotations and generate's complexity, one O(n²) false positive on the fixed
  608×5 layout, train's cognitive complexity 16 against 15).

### Why x is unpredictable: stroke order inside a stage

The painter (catbrush, Stylized Neural Painting's method) fits a whole stage's strokes together; their order in the
list is the order the starting points were drawn at random. Stage order (big to small) is learned (marker loss
0.003); the order inside a stage is noise, so "where is the next stroke" cannot be predicted.

Re-ordering changes the picture where strokes overlap (a later stroke covers an earlier one). On 20 paintings:

| re-ordering | picture change |
| --- | --- |
| sort each stage top to bottom (32 px bands), left to right | mean abs 0.0225 (max 0.040), half the painting's own error to the photo (0.046–0.052) |
| another random order | 0.0224 |
| **keep the order of every overlapping pair, sort the rest** (topological sort by position) | **max 1.8 × 10⁻⁷**: the same picture |

The owner's idea (the finished picture tells the order, working back from the top) holds exactly where strokes
overlap: there the visible stroke is the later one. Where they do not overlap, any order gives the same picture, so
those strokes are free to sort. The third row does that. Mean distance from one stroke to the next:

| stage | strokes | overlapping pairs | jump to the next stroke, list order → sorted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 24 | 47.8% | 131 px → 102 px |
| 1 | 64 | 22.4% | 114 px → 62 px |
| 2 | 160 | 9.2% | 128 px → 42 px |
| 3 | 360 | 4.6% | 115 px → 29 px |

Overlap uses a circle of half the stroke's diagonal plus catbrush's 4 px window margin (conservative). No repainting
needed. Not yet used in training.

### Prompt changes (`buyers/stories.py`)

- The WHY and SAYS fields are merged: one or two first-person spoken sentences that give the reason for raising,
  lowering or keeping the offer and the new amount. Two example lines in the prompt (kept by the owner's decision).
- `SETTING`: never a dash in a spoken line.
- Sign is defined by the offer: "+" higher, "−" lower or 0, "0" the same.
- A buyer never names themselves or speaks of themselves in the third person.
- New step `_imagine` after the last round: each buyer still bidding names 1–3 things around the cat at their place
  from the drawing GPT's 345 classes (`buyers/drawable.py`, copied from drawgpt_v2's meta.json) and says one line.
  This is to replace the rule-based `scenes.py`/props.json (tiger + bicycle: bicycle is tagged active +2 and fits
  any item). Display: one layer per class, random place in the layer.
- `check` counts: offer against sign, sign without a move, over budget, an amount said that is not the offer, a buyer
  naming themselves, dashes, imagined things that cannot be drawn.

On the 100 v5 sales (old prompt) the new counts: **1,240 of 6,103 reactions (20.3%) had a "+" or "−" with the offer
unchanged**; amount said differs 1; names self 5 (in SAYS; the third-person cases were in WHY); dashes 13 (12 by
sellers).

E14 (Physics of LMs 3.1)'s "fullname" augmentation (replace he/she/they with the full name) is already in the
knowledge-text prompt ("Always name the subject in full … never just 'they'"); pronouns are 1.1–2.7% of the words in
the 78 knowledge texts. It is for knowledge text, not speech; dialogues keep I and she.

## 4. The owner's reading of the Qwen sale, and what changed

The owner read rounds 1–5 and the imagined scenes of `sale_2000.md`. Rules added to `stories.py` from that reading
(all in the prompts, tests 173/173):

Buyer reactions (`_reactions`):
- the reason names what the seller just said and how it meets or misses the buyer's own concrete situation;
  "that sounds perfect for me", "she sounds even better now", "this isn't the kind of cat I'm looking for" are banned
  (quoted in the prompt);
- a buyer reacts only to the latest answer; earlier answers are already in the offer, even when the seller repeats
  them (five buyers cited FIV again on the kids question);
- an unchanged offer names what was just said, why it does not matter for them, and what they still wait to hear,
  which must not have been answered yet;
- a withdrawal names what was said that rules the cat out; no withdrawal when nothing has;
- every reason agrees with the general cat facts (Andre and Chloe feared FIV for kids and dogs: 7 lines; v5 had 0).

Seller (`_answer`): may say anything about this cat (not judged), but now gets the same general facts and must not
contradict them or hint at a risk they rule out ("be careful since she's FIV positive" to a parent counts).

Imagined scenes (`_imagine`), enforced by `imagine_problem`, with up to two rewrites of the failing buyers and the
scene dropped if it still fails (`check`: imagine_retried, imagine_dropped):
- every thing must be exactly one of the 345 drawable classes;
- every thing must be named in the line with what it is for in this buyer's life (owner's examples are in the
  prompt: couch + alarm clock for long hours, picture frame + couch for the lost old cat);
- the line may name no other drawable class (everyday-word classes such as eye, key, house are ignored) and nothing on
  `NOT_DRAWABLE` (window, blanket, sofa, rug, bowl, toy, …; not complete, extended while spot-checking).
On the old sale all 8 scenes fail, each for the reason the owner gave.

Not yet enforced: over-budget offers (3 in the sale) and reaction rewrites (waiting for the owner).

Next: re-run seed 2000 with the new prompts (`tools/kaggle_dialogues/dialogues.py`, SALES = 1; update dataset
`alinachuang/buyer-stories-code` from `ml/data/kaggle/buyer_stories_code/` first), owner reads it against the old one.
